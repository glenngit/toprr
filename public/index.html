About toprr

A deployable TypeScript app that fetches the daily Top 10 movies and TV shows from your chosen streaming services, deduplicates them into a single feeding list, and requests genuinely new titles — never re-requesting anything already in your library.

DataStreaming Availability API (movieofthenight.com)
RequestsRadarr / Sonarr directly — Seerr (Overseerr/Jellyseerr) is optional
EnrichmentTMDB (posters, score, language, seasons — optional)
